# Evaluator comparison (development diagnostic)

> Development diagnostic set, written while investigating known evaluator failures. It is small and not independently sampled, so these numbers are NOT an unbiased accuracy benchmark or independent validation.

Denominators: labeled judgments are (claim, condition) pairs whose claim matches an expected fact; they depend on the extracted claims, so they differ between runs unless extraction is reused (`--reuse-extraction`). Failed judgments remain in the denominator as incorrect. Distractor-citation rates use valid judgments only; failed judgments have no usable evidence, so their citation behaviour is reported as unassessed rather than as citing nothing. Unmatched claims are listed in each run's report.md for manual review. All metrics are recomputed from each run's saved records.

Comparable configuration (frozen cases/labels, prompts, schemas, options, conditions): **yes**
